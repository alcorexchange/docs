title: "Farms"

A farm (an "incentive" in the contract) pays a set amount of a reward token to the liquidity positions of one Alcor Swap pool over a fixed period. Projects create farms to attract liquidity to their pools. Liquidity providers stake their positions in a farm to earn its rewards on top of swap fees.

Farms run entirely on-chain, in the `swap.alcor` contract.

## Farming in the app

Farms are at `alcor.exchange/v/<chain>/swap/farms`, where `<chain>` is `wax`, `xpr`, `vaulta` (EOS) or `telos`.

- **My Farms**, **All** and **Ended** filter the list. Search by token, contract or reward, and **Hide low TVL** hides small pools.
- Each pool shows its farms with APR, end date, total staked, and your share, daily reward and earned amount.
- If you have no position in a farmed pool, **Add liquidity** opens the [new position form](/alcor-swap/providing-liquidity). When the pool has running farms, the form stakes the new position in them in the same transaction (you can opt out).
- **Stake All** stakes every in-range position you have that is not yet staked in a running farm. **Claim All** claims all your rewards.

You can also stake, claim and unstake from a [position's page](/alcor-swap/pool-page#position-page).

## Rules

- **Only in-range positions can be staked.** A position's staking weight is √(amount A × amount B) of the tokens it holds. A position outside its range holds only one token, so its weight is zero and the contract refuses to stake it.
- The weight is calculated when the position is staked, and recalculated when liquidity is added to or removed from it.
- A farm releases its rewards evenly over its duration. At every moment, each staked position gets a share in proportion to its weight.
- Rewards can be claimed at any time. Unstaking also pays out the rewards earned so far.
- A position can be staked in up to 15 farms at once.
- A position must be unstaked from all farms before it can be [locked permanently](/alcor-swap/locking-liquidity).
