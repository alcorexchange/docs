---
title: "Technical Details"
description: "How farms work inside the swap.alcor contract."
---

Farms are part of the `swap.alcor` contract. Rewards are calculated on-chain, without a backend.

## Tables

- `incentives`: one row per farm: creator, pool, reward token and amount, duration, end time, reward rate, total staking weight and number of stakes.
- `stakingpos`: for each staked position, the list of farms it is staked in.
- `stakes`: scoped by farm ID; one row per staked position, with its staking weight and rewards earned.
- `incentivefee`: the farm creation fee for accounts that are not token issuers.

## Actions

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `newincentive` | Creates a farm for a pool. |
| `stake(incentiveId, posId)` | Stakes a position in a farm. |
| `stakelastpos(incentiveId)` | Stakes the newest position of the farm's pool; used to open and stake a position in one transaction. |
| `getreward(incentiveId, posId)` | Pays out the rewards earned so far. |
| `unstake(incentiveId, posId)` | Unstakes the position and pays out its rewards. |

Rewards are paid from the `reward.alcor` account, which holds the farms' reward tokens.

## Reward calculation

The calculation follows the Synthetix staking-rewards model:

- **Reward rate** = reward amount / duration, paid per second until the end time.
- **Staking weight** of a position = √(amount A × amount B), from the token amounts the position holds when it is staked. It is recalculated when liquidity is added to or removed from the position. An out-of-range position holds one token only, has zero weight and cannot be staked.
- Each staked position earns the reward rate × its weight / the total weight of the farm, for every second it is staked.
- When the creator adds rewards, the new amount plus the undistributed remainder is spread over a new full duration from that moment.
- A position can be staked in at most 15 farms at once.
