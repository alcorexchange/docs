title: "Alcor Swap"
description: "Alcor's concentrated-liquidity AMM: swapping, liquidity positions, ranges and the on-chain price oracle."
---

Alcor Swap is the AMM of Alcor Exchange. It runs as the `swap.alcor` contract on WAX, XPR Network, EOS and Telos, and works like Uniswap v3: liquidity providers choose a price range for their liquidity, and every pool has a fixed fee tier.

In the app, swapping is at `alcor.exchange/v/<chain>/swap` and liquidity is under the **Pool** tab at `alcor.exchange/v/<chain>/swap/positions`. `<chain>` is `wax`, `xpr`, `vaulta` (EOS) or `telos`.
