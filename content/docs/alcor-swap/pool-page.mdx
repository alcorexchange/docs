The **Pool** tab of the swap card opens your positions at `alcor.exchange/v/<chain>/swap/positions`. The page has three tabs.

## Positions

- **Summary**: total value, PnL, APR (fees and farming), how much of your liquidity is in range, fees earned in the last 24 hours, and unclaimed fees.
- **Position list** (cards or table): pair and fee tier, price range, token amounts, value, farm rewards, fees and pool share. Sort by value, fees or newest, or search by token.
- **Claim All** collects the fees and farm rewards of all your positions in one transaction.
- **New** opens the [new position form](/alcor-swap/providing-liquidity).

## Position page

Click a position to open `alcor.exchange/v/<chain>/swap/positions/<id>`. From there you can:

- add liquidity to the position, at the same range;
- remove liquidity (25%, 50%, 75%, 100% or any share), which also collects its fees;
- collect fees;
- stake the position in the pool's farms, claim rewards and unstake;
- [lock the position](/alcor-swap/locking-liquidity) until a date or permanently;
- see the position's history of adds, removals and fee collections.

## Farms and Activity

- **Farms** opens the [farms](/alcor-farms-unlocking-defi-opportunities) page.
- **Activity** lists your liquidity history across all pools: adds, removals and fee collections, each linked to its transaction.
