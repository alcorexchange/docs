---
title: "How to Add Liquidity on Alcor Exchange"
---

1. Navigate to [Alcor Exchange](https://wax.alcor.exchange/)
2. Click on "Pool"

![](/images/uCit3In9aVypPAS7IV5b-image.png)

3. Click on "New position"

![](/images/y3IVe5YgaXpXoVEHlO1Q-image.png)

4. Select your desired pair

![](/images/0wNGIEX5HVYri5VrncKb-image.png)

5. Select the fee tier and amount of liquidity you want to contribute

![](/images/HC6c8LUS3eEL3xXYpGhn-image.png)

6. Click "Add Liquidity"

![](/images/MOMIjZWdmghRg13XsgSI-image.png)

7. Approve the transaction

![](/images/l9gzlb4Z6VhB2K8VKgbc-image.png)

8. That´s it! You can track your positions on ["Pools"](https://wax.alcor.exchange/positions)

![](/images/MnLipIT5eSlD2dH23wKZ-image.png)
