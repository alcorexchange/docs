---
title: "How to set a Range for your Liquidity position"
---

Setting a Price Range has been designed to be easy! Here is a step-by-step guide on how to set up a price range. 

1. [Provide liquidity](/alcor-swap/providing-liquidity) to any pair

![](/images/vYorob7M8zy6grCYWuHF-image.png)

2. Head over to "Set Price Range"

![](/images/F7UAzhP5RFt9DPh67oEr-image.png)

3. Use the Red and Green Bars to determine your Liquidity Range. You can also use "Min Price" and "Max Price below"

![](/images/2zd4dYNqe1FFbD4M1I1C-image.png)

4. Once you have chosen your ideal Liquidity Range, complete the process by choosing how much Liquidity you want to provide. 

![](/images/5Kxg7W4aFB2fs9FVXjYB-image.png)

5. Sign the transaction and that´s it! You can track your positions on ["Pools"](https://wax.alcor.exchange/positions)
