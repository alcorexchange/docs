The swap page is at `alcor.exchange/v/<chain>/swap`, where `<chain>` is `wax`, `xpr`, `vaulta` (EOS) or `telos`.

## Making a swap

1. Connect your wallet.
2. Pick the token to sell in **Sell** and the token to get in **Buy**, then enter an amount in either field. If you type in **Buy**, the swap is calculated for that exact output.
3. Check the quote: **Expected Output**, **Price Impact** and **Min. Received** (the least you will get at your slippage setting).
4. Confirm and sign the transaction in your wallet. The swap executes in that transaction.

Alcor finds the route for you. A swap can pass through several Alcor Swap pools, and part of it can be filled on Alcor's order book markets.

## Swap settings

The settings menu on the swap card holds:

- **Slippage %**: how far the result may fall below the quote before the transaction fails. Choose Auto, 0.5%, 1% or type your own value.
- **Max Hops**: the most pools a route may pass through (1, 2 or 3).
- **Recalculate On Price Change**: refresh the quote when the pools on the route change.
- **Split Into Order Book**: allow part of the swap to be filled on order book markets.
