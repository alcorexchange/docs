**Prerequisites:**

* Ensure you have a valid WAX wallet with the required USDT balance.
* The deposit destination should be a Centralized Exchange (CEX) such as Binance or KuCoin.

**Instructions:**

1. **Select EOS Network for Deposit:**

![](/images/rk6cSC7JqlWjvcYNlPVb-image.png)

* When depositing USDT to your chosen CEX, ensure that you select the EOS network as the source. This is a critical step for a successful transaction.

1. **Read Carefully:**
   * Carefully read the instructions provided here before initiating the deposit. Pay attention to details such as the CEX deposit address and the memo for deposits.
2. **Include CEX Deposit Details:**

![](/images/sLk3NR00hNS0vRub0MdH-image.png)

* Make sure to include the correct CEX deposit address and the associated memo during the deposit process. This information is essential for proper fund crediting.

3. **Deposit Fee:**

* Note that there is a deposit fee of 0.05 USDT. Account for this fee in your deposit amount.

4. **Fill in MEMO on Exchange:**

* On the exchange platform, fill in the MEMO exactly as provided above. Accuracy in entering the MEMO is crucial to avoid potential fund loss.
* Be aware that the MEMO may vary between different wallets or exchanges. Always refer to the interface for the correct MEMO associated with your transaction.

5. **Double-Check Information:**

* Before confirming the deposit, double-check that you've included the correct CEX deposit address, memo, and accounted for the deposit fee.

6. **Complete Deposit:**

* Confirm and complete the deposit process on the WAX blockchain.

**Important Note:**

* An incorrectly filled MEMO can lead to the loss of funds. Exercise caution and verify all details before finalizing the deposit.
