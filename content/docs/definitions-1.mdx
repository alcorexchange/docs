---
title: "Definitions"
description: "Common trading terms used on Alcor"
---

**Mainnet** — the live, production network of a blockchain, as opposed to a testnet.

**Market order** — an order to buy or sell immediately at the best available price. It is guaranteed to execute, but the execution price is not guaranteed.

**Limit order** — an order to buy or sell at a set price or better. It waits in the order book until it is filled or cancelled.

**Order book** — the list of open buy (bid) and sell (ask) limit orders for a market, grouped by price.

**Liquidity pool** — a pool of two tokens that traders swap against instead of matching with another trader. See [What is a liquidity pool?](/faq/what-is-a-liquidity-pool)

**Fee tier** — the fee a pool charges on every swap, paid to its liquidity providers. Alcor pools use 0.01%, 0.05%, 0.3% or 1%.

**Price impact** — how much your own trade moves the price of the pair. It is larger in pools and markets with little liquidity.

**Slippage** — the difference between the expected price of a trade and the price at which it executes. The slippage tolerance you set on a swap defines the minimum amount you accept to receive; if the price moves beyond it, the swap fails instead of executing.
