For applications, use the [Alcor SDK](https://github.com/alcorexchange/alcor-v2-sdk). The formulas below are for working with the contract directly.

## Pool price

`sqrtPriceX64` is in the `currSlot` field of the pool row. `precisionA` and `precisionB` are the token precisions.

```
Price of tokenA in tokenB:
sqrtPriceX64 ** 2 / 2 ** 128 * 10 ** (precisionA - precisionB)

Price of tokenB in tokenA:
2 ** 128 / sqrtPriceX64 ** 2 * 10 ** (precisionB - precisionA)
```

`sqrtPriceX64` is a 128-bit integer. Square it with big integers (e.g. `BigInt`) to avoid losing precision.
