`book.alcor` is Alcor's spot order book contract: price-time priority, limit orders with post-only, IOC and FOK,
market buys by amount, order expiry, stop and take-profit triggers with OCO, and swaps straight from a wallet with
one transfer. The swap router reads it together with the AMM pools, so a swap on Alcor fills against the order book
whenever its price is better.

## Internal balances

Trading runs on balances held by the contract:

1. **Deposit** — transfer a token to `book.alcor` with any memo. The amount is credited to the sender.
2. **Trade** — orders lock what they need from the available balance and trade without token transfers.
3. **Withdraw** — `withdraw` sends tokens from the available balance to any account that has a row of that token.

A [swap](/developers-api/order-book/swap) skips the ledger: one transfer in, the output and any change come back in
the same action, and nothing stays on the exchange.

Each balance has `available` and `locked`. A sell locks its base; a buy locks `qty * price` plus the taker fee and
gets back whatever the fill did not need.

## Units

Every market has two fixed numbers:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `lot_size` | minimal base units in one lot |
| `price` | minimal quote units one lot costs |

An order of `qty` lots at `price` moves `qty * lot_size` base against `qty * price` quote. All math is in integers;
only fees are rounded (down, per fill). `tick_size` is the price step, `min_notional` the smallest quote value of
any order or trigger that rests in the book.

**Example: WAX/USDT on WAX.** WAX has 8 decimals, USDT 6. `lot_size = 10000000000` is 100 WAX. At 0.00456 USDT per
WAX a lot costs 0.456 USDT, so `price = 456000`. `tick_size = 10` makes the price step 0.00001 USDT per lot, or
0.0000001 USDT per WAX. `min_notional = 1000000` is 1 USDT.

`lot_size` never changes after listing. `tick_size` and `min_notional` can.

## Fees

Maker and taker fees are set per market in basis points (`maker_bps`, `taker_bps`). The fee is taken in quote and
rounded down on each fill. The rate is fixed in the order when it is placed, so a later fee change never makes a
resting order more expensive. The maker rate is never above the taker rate. All markets listed so far trade with
0 / 0.

## Limits

- **Work budget.** One action does at most 1000 units of work: a fill, or the removal of one of your own orders or
  an expired order in the way. If the budget runs out while the book still crosses, the rest comes back to you
  (`truncated = true`) and never rests in the book.
- **Minimum size.** A taker remainder below `min_notional` comes back instead of resting. A maker remainder that a
  partial fill leaves below `min_notional` is cancelled and refunded.
- **Self-trade prevention.** Your own order in the way of your taker order is cancelled, not traded.
- **Expiry.** An order can carry `expires_at` (block time, seconds). After it the order no longer trades: a taker
  that reaches it removes it and refunds its owner, and anyone may cancel it.
