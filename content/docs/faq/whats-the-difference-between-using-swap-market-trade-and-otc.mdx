---
title: "What's the difference between Swap, Spot and OTC?"
---

* [**Swap**](/main-page-overview/swap) fills your trade right away at the current price, routed through [liquidity pools](/faq/what-is-a-liquidity-pool) and, by default, order books.
* [**Spot**](/main-page-overview/spot) trades on a market's order book. A limit order waits until another user fills it at your price; a market order fills right away against the open orders.
* [**OTC**](/main-page-overview/otc) (over-the-counter) is a direct trade with another user at a fixed price set by the order's creator, independent of pool and order book prices.
