---
title: "Exchange rate"
description: "How the LSW/WAX exchange rate is calculated and why it changes."
---

The exchange rate is the amount of LSW one WAX buys:

```
Exchange rate = total LSW in circulation / total WAX staked (including rewards)
```

Both totals are in the `stakemints` table of `liquid.alcor` (`totalLiquidStakedToken` and `totalNativeToken`).

- **Staking**: LSW received = WAX deposited × exchange rate.
- **Unstaking**: WAX received = LSW returned / exchange rate.

The rate started at 1. Every time staking rewards are added, the WAX total grows while the LSW total stays the same, so the rate goes down: each LSW is redeemable for more WAX. Staking and unstaking themselves do not change the rate. The staking page shows the current rate.

## Example

1. Alice is the first staker. She stakes 100 WAX and receives 100 LSW.

   ```
   Exchange rate = 100 / 100 = 1
   ```

2. Over time, 10 WAX of rewards are added.

   ```
   Exchange rate = 100 / (100 + 10) ≈ 0.909
   ```

3. Alice unstakes her 100 LSW and receives 100 / 0.909 ≈ 110 WAX.
