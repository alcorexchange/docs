title: "Withdrawal process"
---

![](/images/Wxg0cnWI4bG2FPpKuJbL-image.png)

Users can choose to withdraw their WAX on Alcor which involves the 3-day unbonding time mandated by WAX, or swap their LSW for WAX on Alcor or other listed DEXes for instant withdrawal.

There are currently no fees involved for Liquid Staked WAX.
