---
title: "OTC"
description: "Peer-to-peer trades at a fixed price"
---

OTC (over-the-counter, `/v/<network>/otc`) lets you offer an amount of one token for a fixed amount of another. There is no slippage: whoever fills the order gets exactly the amounts in it. Orders are held by Alcor's OTC contract on the selected network.

## Tabs

* **Orders** — all open orders. Filter by the token being sold (**Selling**) and the token wanted (**For**), or search by token or account.
* **My Orders** — your own open orders.
* **History** — completed trades with maker, buyer, amounts and price.

## Creating an order

Click **Create**, enter what **You sell** and what **You receive**, and confirm in your wallet. The tokens you sell are transferred to the OTC contract and stay there until the order is filled or cancelled.

## Filling or cancelling an order

Open an order to see its price and amounts. **Buy** sends the requested tokens and you receive the offered ones in the same transaction. The maker can **Cancel Order** at any time to get the tokens back.

<Callout type="warn">
Anyone can create an OTC order with any token. Check the token contract before buying; orders that involve a known scam token are marked with a warning.
</Callout>
