Portfolio (`/v/<network>/portfolio`) shows the connected account on the selected network. The header shows the total portfolio value, available funds, funds in liquidity pools and the number of active positions.

## Tabs

* **Balances** — every token you hold, with its price and value. Search by name or contract, or hide small balances. From a row you can **Transfer** the token, open it in Swap or Spot, or — for assets carried by the Alcor bridge — **Deposit** and **Withdraw** them through the bridge. Click a token to open its analytics page.
* **Open Orders** — your open [Spot](/main-page-overview/spot) orders, with **Cancel All**.
* **Liquidity Pools** — your liquidity positions and their unclaimed fees.
* **Owned Farms** — farms you created as an incentive provider.
* **Issued Tokens** — shown only when the account has issued its own tokens.
* **Resources** — CPU, NET and RAM usage, staking and unstaking for resources, and pending refunds. On networks whose system contract pays voters (such as WAX), voting rewards can be claimed here.

<Callout type="info">
Tokens locked in open orders are not part of your available balance until the order is filled or cancelled.
</Callout>
